## HOW TO PICK THE RIGHT PAIR FOR YOUR FRIEND

1. **Ask about their preferred style**  
   - *Casual streetwear?* → AF1, Stan Smith, Reebok Classic.  
   - *Sporty/athletic?* → RS‑X, ML574, Air Max 90.  
   - *Budget‑first, “starter” shoe?* → Bata Ace‑Lite.

2. **Check their foot shape**  
   - *Wide feet* → Try on both the AF1 and ML574 (they have generous toe boxes).  
   - *Narrow feet* → The Stan Smith runs a bit small, so go half‑size up.

3. **Consider colour‑matching**  
   - *Neutral tones* (white, black, navy) are easier to mix‑and‑match.  
   - *Bold colours* (RS‑X, Air Max) are great for “statement” days.

4. **Think about long‑term value**  
   - Leather (AF1, Stan Smith, Reebok) ages beautifully → personal patina.  
   - Synthetic (RS‑X, Ace‑Lite) is lower maintenance and lighter.

5. **Future resale potential (if they ever want to upgrade)**  
   - Nike & Adidas hold value better than local brands.  
   - The ML574 is a “sneaker‑head” staple and often trades above ₹2,500 after a few years.
